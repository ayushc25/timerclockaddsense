Introduction

Most timer apps are built to create urgency. A meditation session needs the opposite — a tool that gets out of the way. The TimerHub Meditation Timer strips things back to what actually matters for a sitting practice: a duration, a moment to settle before the clock starts, a soft bell to mark the beginning and end, and nothing else competing for your attention in between.

How to Use

Choose a session length from the presets, or enter a custom number of minutes. If the settling toggle is on, pressing start begins a brief 10-second preparation countdown with a calm "Settle in..." caption — this is your moment to find a comfortable position before the timer proper begins. A soft bell marks the true start of your session, and the countdown then runs silently until a second bell marks its end. Pause and resume at any time if you're interrupted, and use fullscreen for a completely uncluttered view.

Features

  • Seven duration presets from 5 to 60 minutes, plus any custom length
  • Optional 10-second settling moment before the timer begins, with a calm caption
  • Soft bell tone — not a harsh alarm — at both the start and end of your sitting
  • A single, minimal control set — start, pause, reset — with nothing else to think about
  • Drift-corrected timing that stays accurate even if your screen dims or you switch apps
  • Fullscreen focus view for a genuinely distraction-free sit

Use Cases

Mindfulness practice. A fixed, unobtrusive sitting length so you're not wondering how much time is left. Breathing exercises. Time a box-breathing or extended-exhale practice without a jarring end signal. Guided or silent meditation. Use it alongside a guided recording, or in complete silence with just the two bells. Short mental resets between meetings. A five- or ten-minute reset that gives you a clean break between calls. Sleep wind-down. A longer, gentle session to settle your mind before bed.

Tips

Find a quiet space and, where possible, silence notifications on the device you're using — the timer can handle the counting, but it can't stop other apps from interrupting you. If you're new to meditation, try starting with just 5 minutes; consistency matters far more than length when you're building the habit. Keep the settling countdown on if you tend to rush into sitting down — those ten seconds make a real difference in how present you feel once the bell rings.
