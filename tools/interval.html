Introduction

The TimerHub Interval Timer is built for one job: keeping you moving through work and rest periods without touching your phone. Whether you're following Tabata, running a HIIT circuit, timing EMOM sets, or coaching a group class, it counts every round accurately, announces phase changes with a clear tone, and gives you a 3-2-1 audio cue before each interval ends so you're never caught off guard mid-rep.

How to Use

Pick a preset that matches your workout style, or enter your own work time, rest time, round count and prep countdown. Tap Start Workout and the timer takes over — counting down your prep window, then alternating work and rest until every round is complete. Use Pause if you need to stop mid-set, and Reset to return to setup and change your numbers. Tap the fullscreen icon to blow the display up for viewing across a gym floor or propping your phone against a water bottle.

Features

  • Work, rest, rounds and an optional prep countdown, fully customizable
  • One-tap presets for Tabata, HIIT, EMOM and strength circuits
  • Clear phase badge (Prepare / Work / Rest) with round tracking and a preview of what's next
  • A distinct 3-2-1 beep before every phase change, so you can keep your eyes off the screen
  • Drift-free timing that stays accurate even if your phone screen locks or the tab loses focus
  • Fullscreen focus mode with large, tap-friendly controls built for sweaty hands

Use Cases

HIIT circuits: alternate 30-45 seconds of work with matching rest across 6-10 rounds for a full-body conditioning session. Tabata: the classic 20-seconds-on, 10-seconds-off protocol for 8 rounds — brutal, short, effective. EMOM (Every Minute on the Minute): set work to 60 seconds and rest to 0, then fit your reps inside each minute before the next one starts. Boxing rounds: set work to 3 minutes and rest to 1 minute for standard round timing. Circuit training: string together stations with even work/rest splits. Yoga and mobility flows: use longer work windows with short transition breaks between poses.

Tips

  • Prop your phone in fullscreen mode where you can see it from your workout position — the phase badge color and large digits are readable from several meters away.
  • Use the prep countdown to get into position before the first work interval starts — 10 seconds is usually enough.
  • Set rest to 0 for EMOM-style training where the "rest" is simply whatever time is left in the minute.
  • Turn sound on before you start — the 3-2-1 cue is what lets you keep your head up during the last rep of a set.
