Introduction

The Online Alarm turns any browser tab into a simple wake-up, nap or reminder alarm. There's nothing to install and nothing to configure beyond picking a time — set it, leave the tab open, and TimerHub will ring at the exact moment you chose, with a clear on-screen countdown the whole way there.

It's built for the moments where a full phone alarm app is overkill: a quick reminder while you're already at your desk, a nap timer on your laptop, or a "wake me up" alarm while you work with headphones in. If you need a precise interval instead of a fixed clock time, our Countdown Timer or Timer may suit you better.

How to Use

Pick the time you want the alarm to ring using the time field — if that time has already passed today, TimerHub automatically schedules it for tomorrow and tells you so directly above the picker (for example, "Rings tomorrow at 6:30 AM"). Choose an alarm tone — Classic, Gentle or Urgent — then press Activate Alarm. The card switches to a live countdown showing exactly how much time is left. When the countdown reaches zero, the alarm rings and a prominent Stop Alarm button appears — tap it to silence the alarm and reset. You can cancel an armed alarm at any time with the Cancel Alarm button instead of waiting for it to ring.

Features

  • Set any time of day — TimerHub works out today vs. tomorrow automatically
  • Live, self-correcting countdown to the exact alarm moment
  • Three alarm tone characters — Classic, Gentle and Urgent
  • One-tap cancel while armed, and a clear Stop Alarm control once it rings
  • Honest, visible notice about the tab-open limitation of browser alarms
  • Nothing to install and nothing sent to a server — it all runs locally

Use Cases

People use the Online Alarm to wake themselves up from a short nap at their desk, to set a reminder to check on something cooking on the stove, to give themselves a hard stop for a study session, or as a simple medication reminder while they're already sitting at the computer. It's also handy as a backup alarm alongside your phone when you want a second, independent way to be reminded of something at a specific time.

Tips

Because this alarm depends on the tab staying open, it works best as a desk or desktop companion rather than a replacement for your phone's alarm when you're away from your computer. Keep the tab pinned so you don't accidentally close it, and consider using the Gentle tone for naps and the Urgent tone for anything you really can't afford to sleep through. If you just need to know when a fixed amount of time has passed rather than a specific clock time, the Timer is a better fit.
