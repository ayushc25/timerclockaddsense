Introduction

The Lap Timer is built around one idea: the lap list is the point, not an afterthought. Instead of watching a single running clock, you get a live stats row showing your best lap, your average lap, and your lap count, plus a full split history you can scan the moment you finish a session — ideal for track workouts, swim sets, driving laps or any repeating course.

How to Use

Tap the center button to start, then tap the lap button every time you complete a circuit — a track loop, a pool length, a driving lap. Each tap logs a split and instantly updates your best lap, average lap and lap count above the clock. Pause any time with the center button, and reset (available only while paused) clears the whole session so you can start a fresh set.

Features

  • Live best-lap, average-lap and lap-count stats that update after every split
  • Millisecond-accurate timing with a drift-corrected engine
  • Full lap history with split time and cumulative total for every lap
  • Automatic best/worst lap highlighting so consistency issues jump out immediately
  • Fullscreen mode for poolside, trackside or dashboard use
  • Optional lap-confirmation click for eyes-free operation

Use Cases

Running splits. Lap every time you pass the same marker on a track or loop, and compare each lap against your average to judge pacing in real time.

Swim laps. Tap at each wall touch to build an accurate length-by-length record without needing a poolside coach to call splits.

Race and driving practice. Lap at the start/finish line on a circuit to track consistency across a practice session, not just your single fastest lap.

Interval-based training. Use laps to mark the end of each rep or round in structured training, then review the full spread afterward.

Tips

Average lap is the most honest signal of overall pace — a single great lap can hide an otherwise inconsistent session, so watch how close your splits sit to the average rather than only chasing the best-lap badge. The lap list shows your most recent lap at the top; scroll down to find lap 1 at the very bottom of the list.
