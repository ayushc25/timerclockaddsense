Introduction

The Digital Clock is a distraction-free, oversized clock that runs entirely in your browser tab. There's no setup beyond opening the page — it reads the correct time straight from your device and updates every second, making it a handy stand-in for a physical desk clock, a lobby display or a classroom wall clock.

It pairs naturally with our other everyday tools: use the Online Alarm when you need to be reminded at a specific time, or the Timer when you need to count down a duration rather than just watch the current time.

How to Use

Open the page and the clock starts immediately — no configuration required. Use the 12-hour / 24-hour toggle to switch between "6:45:12 PM" and "18:45:12" style formats, and the Show / Hide seconds toggle if you'd rather see a calmer display without the seconds ticking over. Tap the fullscreen icon in the top-right of the card to blow the clock up to fill your entire screen — ideal for projecting onto a classroom screen or leaving up on a spare monitor. Press the exit icon or Escape to leave fullscreen.

Features

  • Large, legible display built from your device's own system clock
  • One-tap 12-hour / 24-hour format switch
  • Optional seconds display for a calmer, slower-moving clock
  • Full date shown underneath, in plain language
  • Genuine fullscreen mode for second monitors and projectors
  • No installs, no accounts, no ads inside the clock itself

Use Cases

Keep this open as a desk display next to your monitor, project it fullscreen during a workshop or conference intermission, put it up on a classroom screen so students can see the time at a glance, or dedicate a spare second monitor to it as a simple, timezone-free clock that's always visible. Because it reads your device's own local time, there's nothing to configure for your timezone or daylight saving — it's always correct.

Tips

If you're projecting the clock for a group, switch to 24-hour format to avoid any AM/PM ambiguity, and hide the seconds if the ticking motion is distracting on a shared screen. On a dedicated second monitor, fullscreen mode gives you a clean, borderless clock with nothing else competing for attention.
