Privacy Policy

How TimerHub handles data, in plain language.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

TimerHub is a static, client-side website. There is no user account system, and the tools on this site are designed to run entirely in your browser. This policy explains, honestly and specifically, what that means for your data.

Information We Collect

TimerHub does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name or email address simply by using the timer tools. The only place personal information is submitted is our contact form, which you fill in voluntarily to reach us directly.

How We Use Local Storage

Some tools save small preferences directly in your browser's local storage so the site behaves the way you left it — for example, sound preferences (such as whether alert sounds are on or off, and volume level) are saved to your browser's local storage under a key named timerhub_sound_prefs. This information is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and clearing your browser's site data removes it completely.

Cookies

TimerHub does not currently set tracking cookies. Local storage, described above, is a separate browser mechanism used only to remember your in-app preferences on your own device, not to identify or track you across sites.

Third-Party Services

TimerHub does not currently use any active third-party analytics service. The site includes a technical integration point that could support an analytics provider in the future, but as of this policy's last update, no analytics data is collected or transmitted. If that changes, we will update this policy to name the provider and describe what is collected before it goes live.

Advertising

Some pages reserve visual space for advertising ("Advertisement Space" placeholders). These slots are not currently connected to an active ad network. If TimerHub begins working with an advertising provider in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what data, if any, that provider collects (such as anonymized ad-delivery data), and how you can control it.

Children's Privacy

TimerHub is a general-audience productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the site does not collect personal information through normal use, and any information submitted (such as through the contact form) is provided voluntarily, we do not knowingly gather data from children through the ordinary use of our timers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves — for example, if we introduce an analytics or advertising integration. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above, and we encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how TimerHub handles data, please reach out through our contact page.
